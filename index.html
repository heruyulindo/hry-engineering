About HRY Engineering

HRY Engineering began as an engineering initiative during university, a place to work on real technical problems beyond coursework. It is now being rebuilt as an independent engineering service.

The aim is a practical consultancy that supports individuals, startups, and manufacturing companies with design, analysis, and process-focused engineering work.

How we think

Analytical thinking

Break a problem into measurable parts before proposing a fix.

Technical discipline

Document assumptions, check results, and keep drawings and models clean.

Continuous improvement

Each iteration, whether a design revision or a process change, should teach something.

Concept to application

Engineering theory only matters when it holds up in a real part, process, or product.

Behind HRY Engineering

HRY Engineering is run by Heru Yulindo, a mechanical engineer (Gunadarma University, 2023). His background covers plastic packaging, mold design review and trials, industrial engineering, and simulation with ANSYS, Moldflow and SolidWorks.
